Proof of Concept: A 5-row × 8-column perspective grid rendered over a combat background image. Gold tile borders (0.35 alpha) converge toward upper-center vanishing point. Heroes (F=Fighter, S=Stormcaller, P=Paladin) occupy left-front tiles; Enemies (E1-E4) occupy right-middle tiles. The active tile (row 2, center) pulses gold to demonstrate click-to-target selection. Background source: public/images/menu_bg/menu_wide.jpg — atmospheric fantasy environment serving as reference for the 80/20 playfield-to-sky split.
